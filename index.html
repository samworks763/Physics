0. Hamara Main Maqsad Kya Hai?

Hamare paas ek gol flat disk hai jiske upar electric charge phaila hua hai. Hum ne disk ke center ke exact upar (distance z par) point P par Electric Field (E) ki taqat maloom karni hai.

Step 1: Disk ko Concentric Rings me Torna

Poori disk par ek sath math lagana mushkil hai. Is liye hum isay onion (pyaz) ki tarah concentric rings me divide karte hain. Radius 'r' aur width 'dr' wali ring ka charge 'dq' yeh hoga:

$$dq = \sigma \cdot dA = \sigma (2\pi r \, dr)$$

Step 2: Ek Single Ring ki Electric Field ka Formula

Ek single charged ring apni field dE axis par lagati hai. Note: Right aur left side wale push symmetry ki wajah se aapas me cancel ho jaate hain, sirf vertical (z) push bachta hai!

$$dE = \frac{k \cdot dq \cdot z}{(z^2 + r^2)^{3/2}} = \frac{k \cdot (\sigma 2\pi r \, dr) \cdot z}{(z^2 + r^2)^{3/2}}$$

Step 3: Sab Rings ko Integrate (Jama) Karna

Ab hum integration sign ($\int$) ko sum machine ki tarah istemal karke center ($r = 0$) se le kar disk ke bahar wahi kinare ($r = a$) tak sab rings ki fields ko plus kar dete hain.

$$E = k \sigma (2\pi) z \int_{0}^{a} \frac{r \, dr}{(z^2 + r^2)^{3/2}}$$

Step 4 & 5: U-Substitution aur Final Result

Hum $t = z^2 + r^2 \implies dt = 2r \, dr$ substitute karte hain jisse integration boht simple ho jati hai. Values solve karne par yeh final formula milta hai:

$$\vec{E} = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0} \left[ 1 - \frac{z}{\sqrt{z^2 + a^2}} \right] \hat{z}$$
Real World & Hardware Security

🛡️ Cybersecurity Aur Hardware Engineering Me Iska Kya Kaam?

Physics ka yeh math Computer Hardware aur Hacking/Defense me kitna zaroori hai, aaiye dekhte hain:

💻

Processor Chip Design

CPUs ke andar billions of tiny microscopic circular capacitors hote hain. Unki electric field ki calculation isi formula se hoti hai taake chip overheat ya short-circuit na ho.

🕵️‍♂️

EM Side-Channel Attacks

Hackers CPU chip se nikalne wali Electromagnetic (EM) Radiation ko analyze karke Encryption Keys (AES/RSA) chura lete hain.

🛡️

TEMPEST Shielding

Disk ki exact electric field calculate karke Faraday Cages aur TEMPEST Shielding banayi jaati hai taake military aur bank servers ko EM attacks se bachaya jaye.

📱

Touchscreens & Sensors

Jab distance $z$ boht chota ho ($z \ll a$), to formula $\vec{E} = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$ ban jata hai. Yeh uniform field Capacitive Touchscreens me finger touch detect karti hai.
