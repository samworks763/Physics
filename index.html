0. Bunyadi Cheez: Charged Disk Kya Hoti Hai?

Aap samjho ke ek flat CD ya coin hai jiske poore surface par charge barabar phaila hua hai. Hum ne is disk ke exact center ke upar (distance z par) point P par total Electric Field (E) ki taqat maloom karni hai.

Step 1: Disk ko Chote Rings me Torna (Breakdown)

Poori solid disk par ek sath math lagana mushkil hai. Is liye hum maante hain ke disk buhut saare patle patle gol 'Rings' se mil kar bani hai. Ring ka radius 'r' aur width 'dr' hai.

$$dq = \sigma \cdot dA = \sigma (2\pi r \, dr)$$

Step 2: Aik Single Charged Ring ki Electric Field

Pehle se maloom ring ke formula me hum bas is chote charge 'dq' ki value put karte hain. Note: Side wale components cancel ho jaate hain, bas vertical axis wala component (z) bachta hai!

$$dE = \frac{k \cdot dq \cdot z}{(z^2 + r^2)^{3/2}} = \frac{k \cdot (\sigma 2\pi r \, dr) \cdot z}{(z^2 + r^2)^{3/2}}$$

Step 3: Tamaam Rings ko Center (0) se Kinare (a) tak Jorna (Integration)

Integration ka matlab hai saare rings ki fields ko aapas me plus kar dena. Constant variables ko integration se bahar nikal lete hain.

$$E = k \sigma (2\pi) z \int_{0}^{a} \frac{r \, dr}{(z^2 + r^2)^{3/2}}$$

Step 4 & 5: Simple Integration Solve & Final Result

Hum $t = z^2 + r^2$ substitute karte hain jisse integration boht asaan ho jati hai. Value solve karne par final formula milta hai:

$$\vec{E} = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0} \left[ 1 - \frac{z}{\sqrt{z^2 + a^2}} \right] \hat{z}$$

🛡️ Yeh Real Life Aur Cybersecurity Me Kyun Zarori Hai?

Aap soch rahe honge ke yeh physics ka math Cybersecurity ya Computer Hardware me kahan kaam aata hai? Dehyan se samjhein:

1. Hardware & Chip Design (Microprocessors)

Modern CPU chips aur Transistors ke andar circular micro-capacitors aur metal disks hoti hain. Unki electric field calculate kiye bagair microchips burn ho sakti hain ya leak ho sakti hain.

2. EM Side-Channel Attacks (Cybersecurity)

Hackers CPU ya Hardware Disk se nikalne wali Electromagnetic (EM) Radiation ko analyze kar ke Encryption Keys (AES/RSA) chura lete hain. Disk ki electric field jan na zaroori hai taake **TEMPEST Shielding** banayi ja sakay.

3. Infinite Sheet Approx ($z \ll a$)

Jab distance $z$ boht chota hota hai, to formula $\vec{E} = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$ ban jata hai. Yeh uniform field Touchscreens aur Sensors banane me istemal hoti hai.

4. Point Charge Approx ($z \gg a$)

Jab distance boht door hota hai, poori disk ek chote se Point Charge $E = \frac{k Q}{z^2}$ ki tarah behave karti hai, jo Antenna signals samajhne me help karta hai.
